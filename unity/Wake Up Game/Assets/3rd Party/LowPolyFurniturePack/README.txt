LOW POLY FURNITURE MEGAPACK
240 models - free

FBX/    one .fbx per model, texture embedded
GLB/    one .glb per model, texture embedded
OBJ/    one .obj + .mtl per model, plus the atlas PNG they reference
blend/  every model in one .blend, laid out on a grid

All models are built to real-world scale with the pivot centred in XY and the base on
Z=0, so they drop onto a floor with no offset. Wall, ceiling and hanging pieces use
their own pivot so they snap to the surface they belong on.

One material, one 256x256 palette atlas (FurniturePack_Atlas.png) for the entire pack - so a fully
furnished room is a single draw call. Flat-shaded, no normal maps, nothing to bake.

FBX and OBJ are exported -Z forward / Y up for Unity and Unreal. GLB is Y-up by spec.

Use in personal and commercial projects. Credit appreciated, not required.
